🐕 Pet-Friendly Dining

Hong Kong Policy · mid-2026

The Hong Kong government will implement the pet-friendly restaurant policy by mid-2026, giving special permits to around 500 to 1000 restaurants, so dogs can get the chance to enter the restaurant and accompany their owners during meals. For pet owners, this is a long-awaited opportunity to share dining moments with their furry friends; however, for some people, it has sparked concerns about hygiene, allergies, and public comfort. This audio news aims to explore the upcoming pet-friendly restaurant policy to be implemented in Hong Kong, and the social expectations and challenges that arise from balancing the social needs of pet owners with public hygiene, allergies, and dining comfort.
